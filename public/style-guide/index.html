4. Cross-Surface Do's and Don'ts

DO: Use Primary Amber (`#E67E22`) exclusively for primary buttons and active key highlights across all 4 surfaces.
DO: Maintain the Space Grotesk + Inter pairing for all headings and body copy without inventing ad-hoc typefaces.
DO: Use thin 1px neutral borders (`#E2E8F0`) with 4px/8px corner radii instead of heavy blurry drop-shadows.
DON'T: Use Amber as a large background fill. Amber is reserved strictly for high-signal interactive controls.
DON'T: Mix 3D equipment illustration art inside data-dense tables in Admin HQ. Keep admin tables clean with line icons and text badges.
